# Persona test (2026-10-17)

Three UI-user agents (sonnet) ran the same five tasks one after another on real smart451 data, after `add-multi-select`: a novice (intern, first week, no CAN tools), the boss (head of testing, 10 minutes, wants to show results) and an expert (CANape/INCA/PlotJuggler, 12 years). Condensed by the lead. Viewport 1440 × 723; the shared workspace held Jan's 17 windows, which the testers left alone.

Tasks: T1 first look; T2 speed and motor temperature over the most recent drive; T3 measure a strong acceleration (Δt, Δv); T4 four wheel speeds in one chart; T5 faults and state changes during that drive.

## Liked

- Signal search and the hover card (DBC message and ID, scaling, raw step, SNA, "shown in"); expert: "CANape needs a dialog for that".
- A/B cursors with Δt/Δv in the legend, linked across windows; 3/4 digits.
- Nothing hidden: SNA share, raw/agg with bin size, loading states.
- Event chips with counts, pins on the strip, lines in the charts; BRK_LMP brake release visible 2–4 s before the speed rises.
- Multi-select (expert): Cmd/Shift + `+` gives a four-signal chart in one action.

## Agreed by several testers

1. **"Most recent drive" (3/3):** nothing names a drive; the view starts on an arbitrary minute or squeezes the data into a sliver. Wanted: "latest drive" / "fit to data", a drive headline (duration, max speed). Note: `add-gap-display` finds logger gaps ≥ 2 s, which is the basis for listing drives.
2. **Events only in the view (3/3):** the list shows all 669 events from 10-11 on; rows inside the range only get an accent border. Wanted: an "in view" filter, or jump to the event nearest the cursor.
3. **Where did my window go (novice, boss):** a new window lands at the bottom of 17; the novice had to scroll for it. `setReveal` should scroll it into view: check why it did not (unverified).
4. **Newcomer cues (novice, boss):** A/B/Diff unexplained (the novice's second click moved A; Shift+click is B); no hint that rows can be dragged onto a window; cryptic names (SPEED_DISP, T_ENG, WSPD_FL) and wheel speeds in 1/min.

## Single voices

- Boss (must): share or keep a view: link, PNG/PDF, a title. Persistence is already next in `status.md`.
- Expert (should): measuring helpers: find peak / max slope, snap cursors to samples.
- Expert (data): all DTCs under Faults carry 07:27:12.135, the readout time, not the onset; correlation with a drive is impossible from that.
- Novice: show the description in the list, not only on hover; better default colours for overlapping lines; event text cut off in the narrow list.

## Not app bugs (tool limits or misreadings)

- "Add to" ignored the choice (novice): native `<select>` cannot be clicked by the tool; the expert used it with `form_input` and it worked.
- Drag zoom, wheel zoom, Shift+click cursors, real drags: not drivable with the tool.
- Marker tooltips not found (expert): pins and glyphs have tooltips (events/marks.ts); hover with the tool is unreliable.
- "74+ windows" (boss): window IDs read as a count; the workspace held 17. The boss's `+` clicks added nothing (misclicks).
- Shift range including TRQ_DL (expert): a range takes the rows in between, as specified.
